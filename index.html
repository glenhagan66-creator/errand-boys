Questions

The honest answers.

How do I pay?

Mobile Money to our business number, or cash on delivery for small orders. For "buy-for-me" errands where we're spending our own money upfront, we ask you to pay before we buy — that protects both of us. Every single order gets an order number and a record.

Do you add secret markup on the products?

No. We earn from the delivery fee and the errand fee — not from quietly inflating what you'd pay in the shop. For sourced items we'll show you the receipt if you ask. That's the whole business model: you pay for convenience, not for being kept in the dark.

How fast is delivery?

Within about an hour during normal hours for in-zone deliveries, often much faster. Items we have to source from town take longer and we'll tell you a realistic time upfront. We'd rather quote you 90 minutes and arrive in 40 than the other way round.

What if you bring the wrong thing?

We confirm the item with you before we leave the shop. If we still get it wrong, we swap it or refund you — our problem, not yours. If an item is unavailable we message you with options instead of guessing.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum. One sachet of Milo at midnight is a valid order. The delivery fee is the same whether you order one item or fifteen — so it's worth adding whatever else you're low on.

I run a small business at GCTU. How does the advertising work?

You pay a flat fee, we post your brand on our WhatsApp Status and socials, and we send you proof. We don't take commission on your sales — what you make is yours. The Campus Takeover package also puts your physical flyer in every delivery bag we hand out that week, which is the part nobody else on campus can offer you.

Who is actually behind this?

A small student team at GCTU running it as a real business, not a side hustle that disappears after two weeks. Every order is logged, every purchase has a receipt, and business money is kept separate from personal money. We're building something we want to still be running after we graduate.

Do you deliver outside the listed zones?

Sometimes — message us and we'll quote you. If it's reachable and it makes sense, we'll do it. Our focus right now is GCTU Tesano, Abeka and the hostels around them, so that's where we're fastest and cheapest.
